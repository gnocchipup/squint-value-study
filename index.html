Auto-refine edges

Blocking polygons only. Refine all levels resets every shape to 4 edges, then adds edges (coarse steps first, then fine) until no contour point is farther than the tolerance from its polygon. Refine current shape does the same for the selected polygon alone, so you can refine all levels at a loose tolerance, then pick out individual shapes at a tighter one.
